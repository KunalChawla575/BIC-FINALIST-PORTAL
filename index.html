Winner bank details

A preview of information requested only if the team wins in one of the 29 award categories.

🔒 Opens after results
Not open for submission

The committee will assign the winning category and provide a separate secure bank form after results are announced. The registered mentor must submit and verify the information. The full team prize, after applicable deductions, is intended for one verified bank account; the organiser will confirm the beneficiary and required authorisations before payment.

1 · Award & beneficiary
  • Team code, winning category and award reference
  • Registered mentor contact and verification
  • Proposed account holder and relationship to team
  • Student name, school, class and parent/guardian details
  • Minor and guardian declaration, if relevant
2 · Account details
  • Account holder name, bank and branch
  • Account number and confirmation
  • IFSC and savings/current account type
  • Verification that one account receives the team award
3 · Supporting documents
  • PAN and tax details, if required
  • Masked government identity proof only if officially required
  • Bank passbook front page or cancelled cheque
  • Guardian/beneficiary authorisation where applicable
  • Further documents requested through a secure channel
4 · Payment declaration
  • Accuracy and ownership of beneficiary details
  • Applicable taxes or withholding before payment
  • Payment timeline after complete verification
  • Corrections handled with the finance team
